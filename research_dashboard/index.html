Research communication

Methods, provenance, and publication boundaries

Primary evaluation: stratified 80/20 patient holdout, with development-only cross-validation. Secondary temporal evaluation: development 2010–2013, validation 2014–2015, test 2016–2017. These designs use different populations; score differences do not prove improvement. The primary AUC interval reflects fixed-model test-sample uncertainty, excluding training and model-selection uncertainty.

This explorer reproduces values already published in the repository's whole-cohort results and five cancer case studies. It does not read or distribute source extracts, subgroup tables, individual predictions, or trained models. Values are rounded as published. It is a static research snapshot, with no Snowflake connection or live refresh.

The data are supplied coursework extracts with SEER-like fields, not a verified full-registry population or survivor-only cohort. Original release, export criteria, and follow-up cutoff remain unverified. No clinical utility, causal treatment effect, or external validation is established.
