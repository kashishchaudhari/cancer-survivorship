RETROSPECTIVE RESEARCH MODEL / LOCAL API

Explore a supported record profile

This model estimates 1-, 3-, and 5-year all-cause survival within the supplied research data. It is not externally validated for clinical prognosis and does not recommend treatment. An explicit stage field is used for Liver only; the other four archives do not provide stage. Model inputs reflect recorded features whose clinical availability time is not verified.

Four independent studies · compare the evidence

Each model has its own patients, temporal split and baseline. Differences in C-index do not prove one cancer is easier to treat or one dataset is more clinically reliable.

What prevents treatment recommendations?

No usable stage field is present in these four extracts. TNM schema is not a patient's stage; grade is not stage. Observational treatment recording lacks the information required to identify individual treatment benefits. More complete longitudinal clinical data and a justified causal design would be needed.
